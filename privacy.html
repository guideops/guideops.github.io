Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Guidant Studio is self-hosted software run by a single user on their own computer.

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no server operated by us.
  • Social platform access tokens are stored locally on the user's own machine and are used only to publish the user's own content at the user's request.
  • Content is transmitted directly from the user's machine to the social platforms' official APIs.
